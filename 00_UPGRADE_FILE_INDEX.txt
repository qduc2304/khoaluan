🎯 VALIDATORS CREATED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

backend/validators.js contains 9 functions:

1. validateEmail(email)
   └─ Validates: RFC email format
   └─ Rejects: Invalid format, missing @, no domain

2. validatePassword(password)
   └─ Validates: Minimum 6 characters
   └─ Rejects: Shorter passwords

3. validateFaculty(faculty)
   └─ Validates: Only 4 TBU faculties
   └─ Rejects: Invalid faculties

4. validateRole(role)
   └─ Validates: 6 defined roles
   └─ Rejects: Invalid roles

5. validateScore(score)
   └─ Validates: 0-100 range
   └─ Rejects: Outside range

6. validateDateFormat(date)
   └─ Validates: YYYY-MM-DD format
   └─ Rejects: Other formats

7. validateRequired(obj, fields)
   └─ Validates: All fields present
   └─ Rejects: Missing or empty fields

8. validateTopicStatus(status)
   └─ Validates: 5 valid statuses
   └─ Rejects: Invalid statuses

9. canScoreTopic(status)
   └─ Validates: approved or grading
   └─ Rejects: Other statuses
